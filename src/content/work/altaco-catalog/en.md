ALTACO pilot catalogue with search, filters and material detail pages based on a controlled stock import. Availability and retail prices reflect an imported snapshot, not real-time stock.

Add materials to your selection and create a PDF offer at retail prices including VAT, without discounts. The generator uses the public catalogue snapshot; a manager confirms quantities and sale terms. Separate natural stone and SM Quartz PDF price lists include all available materials in each family. Team access and automatic data updates are planned for the next stage.
