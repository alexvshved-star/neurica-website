A working case: how ALTACO's internal inventory becomes a public catalog
for a client — item selection and PDF spec generation.

This page is a snapshot built from a specific commit (`build_ref`), not a
live connection to ALTACO's service. Data is sanitized
(`data_mode: SANITIZED`): only name, manufacturer, collection, format,
finish, photo, and an availability flag stay public. Stock depth,
purchase prices, and inventory movement never reach the public build.
