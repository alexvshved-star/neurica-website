ALTACO pilot catalogue with search, filters and material detail pages based on a controlled stock import. Availability and retail prices reflect an imported snapshot, not real-time stock.

Add materials to your selection and create a PDF offer at retail prices including VAT, without discounts. The generator uses the public catalogue snapshot; a manager confirms quantities and sale terms. PDF price lists, team access and automatic data updates are planned for the next stage.
