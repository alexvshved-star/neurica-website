Neurica is EONYX's public experimental surface: prototypes, live demos,
visual studies, case studies. Not a corporate site, not a product.

A living lab, but never a second production surface. Static-first,
minimal backend, no critical business logic. Anything that starts
needing an SLA moves to its own surface.
