---
title: What Neurica Is
summary: a living lab, never a second production surface
tags:
  - manifesto
---

There used to be a different project here. Neurica began as an AI platform for psychological support, with its own product, its own character, its own logic. We closed that direction.

The domain stayed. We could have sold it or left it empty. Instead it got a job we had been missing all along.

At EONYX, things keep appearing that have nowhere to live. A prototype worth showing but too early to sell. A visual series made overnight for no client. A two-hundred-line tool that saved a day of work. All of it used to sit on local drives or die in messengers. Now it lives here.

Neurica works like a workbench turned toward the street. Some things on it genuinely run, and you can touch them. Some are frozen, and that is stated plainly. When something grows into a product, it moves into its own space, and its history stays here.

One rule applies here: nothing has to work. But everything marked LIVE does.
